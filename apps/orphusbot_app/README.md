# OrphusBot app

The Flutter app for OrphusBot (DECISIONS D-033): Linux (Omarchy) now, macOS, Android and iOS from the same code. It signs in with **Sign in with another device** and shows your bots, their whole conversations (with the pictures and files they share), and the approvals and questions waiting for you.

Build and run: see "Flutter app" in [docs/DEVELOPMENT.md](../../docs/DEVELOPMENT.md).
